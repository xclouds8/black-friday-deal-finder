How VeriDeal checks a deal. Every price on this board was read by our agent on 27 November 2026 from the store or from a deals source that names the store, and each card says when and where. The discount is computed from the two prices, never typed in. A verdict of NEW LOW means a source calls it the lowest price ever seen; STRONG means a big cut or a big dollar saving; ROUTINE means a real cut, but a modest one. Where a source states a price's history — a previous price, a previous low, a recurring sale price — the card shows it. No deal on this board carries an INFLATED verdict: we list a sale as inflated only with proof that its price sits above its usual price, and we found no such case among the deals we checked.
